Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to common questions

How long will my roof replacement take?

Most standard roof replacements take 3-5 days for an average semi-detached home. Larger properties or complex roofs may take longer. We'll give you a clear timeline in your quote, and we stick to it.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we understand a new roof is a significant investment. We can discuss flexible payment options when we provide your quote. No pressure—we'll work out what suits you.

What happens if it rains during the job?

We check the forecast before starting and plan accordingly. If unexpected rain comes, we cover exposed areas with heavy-duty tarpaulins. Your home stays protected. We've done this hundreds of times in Welsh weather!

Are your workers fully insured?

Absolutely. We carry full public liability insurance and our team is fully covered. We're happy to provide proof of insurance before any work starts. Your peace of mind matters.

Do you clean up after the work?

Every single time. We lay dust sheets, protect your garden, and do a thorough clean-up at the end of each day. When we leave, the only thing different should be your beautiful new roof.

What guarantee do you offer?

All our work comes with a workmanship guarantee. Materials are covered by manufacturer warranties too. If something's not right, we come back and fix it. No arguments, no excuses.

Why should I choose Delston over other roofers?

We're not here to give you a sales pitch. We show up, we're honest about what needs doing, we quote fairly, and we do the job properly. That might sound basic, but in this industry, it's rare. Our reviews speak for themselves.
